Get NowHere is a private 1:1 peer-to-peer chat app. No phone number. No email. Just your Conceal Network wallet.

Identity comes from your Conceal wallet—not a SIM, not an account signup. You add people the way you meet them in real life: each of you scans the other’s QR code so address and payment ID are exchanged without typing. Then you open a chat room when you actually have something to say—work, vacation, a project—and let it end when that chapter is over.

FEATURES:
• 1:1 P2P chat between two peers
• Sign in with a Conceal Network wallet—no phone or email required
• Meet-in-person style contact add: mutual QR scan for address and payment ID
• Chat rooms with an invitation validity period and a total room lifetime
• Rooms labeled by purpose—work, vacation, and whatever matters now
• Encrypted messaging with wallet-based identity
• Lite Conceal wallet: send, receive, and review CCX history
• Privacy-first design with no account harvest for SMS or email

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Create or import a Conceal Network wallet
2. Meet someone and each scan the other’s QR code to exchange address and payment ID
3. Open a room for a topic that matters, with invite and room validity that expire
4. Talk when you need to—then get back to life

WHY GET NOWHERE:
Technology should facilitate, not enslave. Get NowHere is for conversations with a reason and a season—not an endless feed. Stay reachable for what matters; enjoy the rest of your time offline.

SECURITY & PRIVACY:
• No phone number required
• No email required
• No traditional social-account signup
• Wallet-based identity on Conceal Network
• Open development with documentation you can inspect
