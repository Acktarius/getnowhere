Get NowHere is a **chat app**. No phone number. No email. Only a Conceal
wallet.

You add people like you would in real life, when you meet and exchange a
phone number—except here you flash a QR code. Address and payment ID change
hands without typing. Then you open a room when you actually have something
to say—work, vacation, a project—and let it end when that chapter is over.

Rooms have an **invitation window** and a **total lifetime**. They are labeled
by purpose, not by an endless inbox. Talk when it matters. Enjoy the rest of
life offline. Technology should facilitate, not enslave.

<Screenshots />

## How it works

1. Create or import a Conceal Network wallet
2. Meet someone and each scan the other’s QR code
3. Open a room for a topic that matters, with invite and room validity that expire
4. Talk when you need to—then get back to life
