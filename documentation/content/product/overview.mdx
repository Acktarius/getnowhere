Get NowHere is a private **1:1** chat between two people. Identity is a
Conceal Network wallet, not a SIM and not an account signup. The name reads
both ways: *now here*, and *nowhere*.

## Meet like real life

Open **Add contact**. You add people like you would in real life, when you
meet and exchange a phone number—except here you flash a QR code. Each of
you shows one; each of you scans the other. Address and payment ID go both
ways. One scan is not a contact.

That is still not a chat room. A room comes after, with an invite window and
a lifetime. There is no public directory and no “join this named room on the
internet.”

See [getting started](/getting-started) for downloads and the first-hour
path.

## Rooms with a reason and a season

A chat is a **room**, not a forever feed. You pick a purpose—work, vacation,
whatever matters now—and you set:

- an **invitation validity** (the window to accept)
- a **room lifetime** (when that chapter ends)

When the topic is done, the room is done. Open another if life needs one.
Stay reachable for what matters; spend the rest of your time offline.

## Wallet in the same app

The lite Conceal (CCX) wallet is how you exist here: create or import, sync,
send, receive, and read history. The app does not offer deposit or withdrawal
*creation*, even if related transactions can show up after sync.

Your seed stays in memory for the session. Browser development uses web
storage; a native secure adapter is the intended mobile direction.

## What this is not

- Not a phone-number or email messenger
- Not a public chat lobby
- Not an endless social feed
- Not an anonymity network — see [privacy](/product/privacy-model)
