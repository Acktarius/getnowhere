Send contact recipient picker

TL;DR

When you send CCX, you can pick a contact from a dropdown instead of pasting their address. The app fills their Conceal address and, when you have it, the payment ID they gave you so they can recognize the transfer. Each contact row shows their name and a small round letter badge.

What you'll get

  • A Contact control at the top of the Send sheet (hidden if you have no usable contacts).
  • Contacts listed A→Z by name, one line each: name on the left, round letter mark on the right.
  • Letter mark: two initials for multi-word names; up to three letters for a single name.
  • Pick → address filled; payment ID filled from their paymentIdTo when you have one, otherwise left blank.
  • Archived and blocked contacts do not appear.
  • You can still type or scan an address by hand.

How it works

The list reads your local contacts (same ones as the Contacts screen). Choosing a row copies address and optional payment ID into the send form. Nothing about how the chain transaction is built changes — only how the form is filled. 

flowchart LR
  A[Open Send] --> B{Eligible contacts?}
  B -->|no| C[Address fields only]
  B -->|yes| D[Contact dropdown]
  D --> E[Pick contact]
  E --> F[Fill address]
  E --> G{Have PidTo?}
  G -->|yes| H[Fill payment ID]
  G -->|no| I[Leave payment ID empty]

What changes for you

  • On Wallet → Send, look for Contact above the address field.
  • Pick a name to autofill; edit fields afterward if you need to.

Risks and open questions

  • Re-picking a contact overwrites address and payment ID (including anything you typed by hand).
  • Photo avatars are not in this change — letter badges only for now.

Out of scope

  • Custom contact photos or uploaded icons.
  • Search / typeahead filtering.
  • Autofill of amount.
  • Changes to spend / dust / chain building.
