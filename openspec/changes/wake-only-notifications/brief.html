Wake-only notifications

TL;DR

People only see a notification after they open the app, because we waited for wallet sync so we could put the message text on the lock screen. After this change, a wake is a separate ping: generic words, no names, no preview, and no need to unlock the wallet.

What you'll get

  • One generic wake (what the live poke gateway already shows: “Get NowHere” / “New message”) when an invite is accepted or a room gets a chain message.
  • A later chain message, five minutes or more after the last wake, can ping again. Sooner than that, it will not.
  • The other person’s wake address stays on the room when the app rewrites the room list, and it is saved even if that room row did not exist yet.
  • “You received a room invite” still appears only after that phone syncs (we do not have a wake address yet).
  • The app icon pin can still appear and still clears when you open the app. Opening the app will not wipe the lock-screen entries.

How it works

When Bob accepts Alice’s invite, his app asks the poke gateway to wake her. When someone sends the first chain message after live chat drops — or another after five minutes — the same gateway sends a generic Apple (or ntfy) alert using the same request the gateway already understands. No gateway redeploy. The phone shows that alert without opening the wallet. Decrypting the actual message still happens later, on the device. 

flowchart LR
  sender[Sender device] -->|poke kind| gateway[Poke gateway]
  gateway -->|generic alert + badge 1| lock[Lock screen]
  sync[Wallet sync later] -->|invite received only| local[Local generic banner]
  sync -->|no preview banner| unread[In-app unread]

What changes for you

  • Settings: “Wake contact” means accepted-invite + new-message pings, not “relay only”.
  • Settings: “Notification banner” is only the local “you received a room invite” line.
  • Testers: background Alice, have Bob accept or send L1′ — expect the generic wake without opening her wallet.

Risks and open questions

  • Review missed that the app was throwing away the wake address when it rewrote the room list. Without that address the ping is never sent, even when the servers are up.
  • If Apple registration is wrong on a build, wakes still will not arrive. This change does not re-audit tokens.
  • Apple and our gateway see which of the two wake types it was — not who or what was said.
  • First invites are not instant if the app is dead. That is intentional.

Out of scope

  • A global device wake address so first invites can ping instantly
  • Making iOS background refresh instant
  • Putting message text or names on the lock screen
  • Turning wake on by default
