Appearance contrast and accents

TL;DR

Dark and Light feel like a flat slab today — almost black, or almost white. After this change the page is a softer charcoal or paper grey, and cards sit clearly on top of it. The little topic icons in a chat room are easier to see. Sky and Pink are extra accent dots only — they color buttons and those icons, not the page.

What you'll get

  • Dark page is charcoal, not near-black. Cards are a clearly lighter tile.
  • Light page is paper grey, not near-white. Cards stay white.
  • Chat-room topic symbols are brighter (same pictures, stronger color).
  • Accent dots add Sky and Pink next to Teal, Blue, Amber, and Violet. Accents only — not new themes.

How it works

Theme is only Dark, Light, or System. That is what paints the page and cards. Accent is the highlight on buttons and room icons. Sky and Pink join Teal / Blue / Amber / Violet. They never become a page theme and they never tint the background. 

flowchart LR
  A[Theme: Dark / Light / System] --> B[Page and cards]
  C[Accent: Teal Blue Amber Violet Sky Pink] --> D[Buttons and room icons]
  B --> E[Softer page, cards still pop]
  D --> F[Sky or Pink highlight]

What changes for you

  • Settings → Appearance: two new color dots labeled Sky and Pink.
  • Every screen looks a bit less harsh in Dark and Light.
  • Room backgrounds show the topic icons more clearly.

Risks and open questions

  • If Dark still feels too grey or too bright, we can nudge the hex values — we will not invent a new theme to fix that.
  • Topic icons are meant to be visible, not loud. If they feel like wallpaper, say so.
  • Older app builds that see a stored Sky or Pink value will fall back to Teal until they update.

Out of scope

  • A full pink or sky “skin” for the whole app
  • Changing Teal, Blue, Amber, or Violet themselves
  • Redesigning chat bubbles beyond inheriting the new colors

Work overview
