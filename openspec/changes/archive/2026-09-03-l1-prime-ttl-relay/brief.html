Cheap / auto-destroy chain messages

TL;DR

When chat has to go over the chain, a normal tap still sends a paid message that is saved forever. A two-second press on send offers two cheaper options that vanish after 60 minutes or 6 minutes. Those never get mined, so you do not pay, and the bubble disappears from both phones.

What you'll get

  • Same chain-link send button as today for a paid, kept message.
  • Long-press shows 60 min (top), 6 min (middle), paid send (bottom).
  • The cheap ones still wake the other person if poke is on.
  • When the timer ends, the text is gone from both chat rooms — not left on the chain.
  • Privacy of the ring stays the same: no thinner mix, no dust shortcuts.

How it works

Live chat is unchanged. Only when send already means “via chain” does long-press appear. The cheap options put the same chat text in the mempool with a self-destruct time. Conceal drops it when that time hits. This app also deletes the bubble on both sides and never stores those rows in the lasting room history. 

flowchart TD
  A[Chain fallback composer] --> B{Press}
  B -->|tap| C[Paid durable message]
  B -->|long-press 2s| D[Flyout]
  D --> E[60 min cheap]
  D --> F[6 min cheap]
  D --> C
  E --> G[Mempool only]
  F --> G
  G --> H[Timer ends]
  H --> I[Gone from chain and both rooms]

What changes for you

  • In a chain-fallback room, hold send to pick 60 min or 6 min.
  • Cheap bubbles show a small timer mark, then disappear. No “expired” leftover.
  • You still need enough unlocked coins to build the cheap send; you get them back when it expires.

Risks and open questions

  • If the other person opens the app after the timer, they will not see the text. They may still get a wake. Use tap if the words must last.
  • If a node refuses self-destruct messages, send fails. The app will not secretly send a paid one instead.
  • If the room itself has already ended, there is nothing to show; a leftover pending row in the wallet until the timer is fine.

Out of scope

  • View-only wallets.
  • A custom timer slider.
  • A countdown clock or expired placeholder.
  • Changing live chat or the poke wake itself.
