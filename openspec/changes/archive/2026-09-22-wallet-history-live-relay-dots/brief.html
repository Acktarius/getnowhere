Wallet history live + relay dots

TL;DR

While the wallet is catching up with the chain, new transactions show up in history as they are found — you should not need to mash the sync arrows. Long lists are split into pages of 25. Relay (L1′) smartmessage rows get their own colored dot; tap it to open the chat room, or the contact if the room is gone.

What you'll get

  • History that updates during sync, not only after a full manual resync.
  • Pages of 25 transactions with Prev / Next when there are more than 25.
  • If you already flipped to page 2+, sync will not yank you back to page 1.
  • A new colored dot on relay / execute smartmessage rows.
  • Tap that relay dot → related chat if it still exists, otherwise the contact.

How it works

Sync already folds chain batches into memory. This change also pushes that mapped history into the app store on a gentle throttle so the Wallet screen can redraw. Pagination is local (same full list, shown 25 at a time). Relay dots come from peeking the smartmessage body; navigation looks up the room id, then falls back to the contact that owned that room. 

flowchart TD
  S[Chain sync batch] --> P[Throttle publish to wallet store]
  P --> L[Wallet history list]
  L --> Page{More than 25?}
  Page -->|yes| Nav[Prev / Next pages]
  Page -->|no| All[Show all]
  L --> Dot{Relay smartmessage?}
  Dot -->|yes| Click[Tap colored dot]
  Click --> Room{Room exists?}
  Room -->|yes| Chat[Open chat]
  Room -->|no| Contact[Open contact]

What changes for you

  • On Wallet, watch history fill in while the height counter climbs.
  • Use the footer controls when history is long.
  • Tap a relay-colored dot to jump into the conversation (or contact).

Risks and open questions

  • Phones are the slower UI; publishes are throttled so catch-up does not jank the list.
  • If neither room nor contact can be found for a relay, the tap does nothing (no toast in this change).

Out of scope

  • Infinite scroll instead of pages.
  • Making invite create / register / revoke dots clickable.
  • Changing how fast the chain sync engine itself runs.
