P2P message retention

TL;DR

Chats should still be there after you leave the app — whether you tap Exit or just hop out like Telegram. Live (Holepunch) history is saved when P2P message retention is on (default). Messages you paid to send on chain always come back while the room is still open.

What you'll get

  • Settings rename: P2P message retention, on by default.
  • Off means live chats are session-only. Paid chain sends still show in the room.
  • Leaving via home / app switcher still keeps live chats (if the setting is on), even if the OS later kills the app.
  • Exit still locks the wallet; next unlock restores the same history rules.
  • When a room expires or is revoked, live history and local chain-relay copies for that room are removed.

How it works

Live bubbles stay instant. In the background we rewrite the encrypted wallet about a second after live send or receive (so a burst does not encrypt the file on every message). Hide and Exit save immediately. Paid chain sends are stored at send time. Incoming chain messages are rescanned. Unlock rebuilds the thread from both sources. We keep sealed live frames on this device — not a shared Keet-style log — so a room that expires or is left can actually drop its local copy. 

flowchart TD
  A[Unlocked chat] -->|L2 send or receive| B[Save live history if retention on]
  A -->|Home / hide| B
  A -->|Exit| C[Save then lock wallet]
  B -->|OS kills app| D[Unlock]
  C --> D
  D --> E[Show live history if retention on]
  D --> F[Always show paid chain sends]
  A -->|Room expires or revoke| G[Wipe that room's local chat copies]

What changes for you

  • You do not need to tap Exit just to keep a live chat.
  • Exit still means lock and unlock next time.
  • The privacy toggle only controls live (P2P) history, not paid chain sends.

Risks and open questions

  • Live text history stays small and is wiped when the room ends (default 7 days, at most 30).
  • Turning the setting off does not instantly delete old live copies; they just stop showing. They go away when the room ends.
  • If you were offline, this phone cannot pull missed live messages from the other phone. That is a later change. Paid chain fallback still works.

Out of scope

  • A second place to store chats besides the encrypted wallet.
  • Changing how chain messages are formatted or what they cost.
  • Catching up history from the peer over the network.
