Stabilize Holepunch fallback

TL;DR

After Alice and Bob accept a chat, Holepunch retries will no longer make the room briefly look unaccepted or disable chain messaging. Connection attempts will be bounded, and the screen will clearly separate the Holepunch connection from the temporary chain fallback.

What you'll get

  • The composer stays enabled after acceptance, including while Holepunch connects or times out.
  • Room state never falls back to pending because stale bootstrap data was loaded.
  • Periodic refresh cannot stack hundreds of simultaneous connection attempts.
  • Holepunch receives up to 120 seconds for DHT peer discovery.
  • Status copy says Holepunch is connecting and that messages temporarily use chain fallback.
  • Linux desktop can warn that active UFW may be interfering after a Holepunch timeout.
  • LAN troubleshooting explains UDP, internet/DHT bootstrap, topics, and firewalls.

How it works

Acceptance becomes monotonic: stale pending data cannot overwrite a post-accept room. All callers for one room share a single active Holepunch attempt, and failures are saved so reloads preserve the correct relay-eligible state. True unaccepted invites remain blocked. On Linux, Electron also performs a privilege-free, best-effort UFW check and passes only a simple advisory to chat diagnostics. 

accepted → Holepunch connecting → connected
    │              │
    └── chain fallback enabled ──→ connect failed
                                      │
                                      └── chain fallback + bounded retry

What changes for you

No new setup or protocol is introduced. During a slow or failed Holepunch connection, you can keep sending grey chain messages. The connection label and fallback hint will describe those as separate paths. If Electron detects active UFW after a timeout, diagnostics will explain that dynamic UDP may be blocked without asking for administrator access.

Risks and open questions

  • A longer deadline means the explicit timeout appears later, but chain messaging remains usable.
  • Holepunch still cannot guarantee a LAN connection when UDP or DHT bootstrap traffic is blocked.
  • Active UFW is a clue, not proof of the failure; the warning remains conditional.
  • Final acceptance needs two real machines because a local browser test cannot reproduce every firewall and NAT.

Out of scope

  • Sending chain messages before an invite is accepted.
  • Adding LAN-only mDNS discovery or a relay network.
  • Changing firewall rules, invoking sudo, or determining a fixed Hyperswarm UDP port.
  • Changing bridge messages, topic derivation, or encryption.
