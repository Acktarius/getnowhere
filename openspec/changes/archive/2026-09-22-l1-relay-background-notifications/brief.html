L1′ relay background notifications

TL;DR

When someone sends you a grey-bubble chain-relay chat while you have Get NowHere open but are not looking at that room, you will see notification pins — on the chat row, the contact row, and each room on the contact screen. The wallet keeps checking the chain in background (slower, to save battery) until you Exit.

What you'll get

  • Background sync: unlocked wallet keeps ingesting L1′ relays even when the tab/window is hidden (30s poll vs faster when visible).
  • Chats list: relay pin on the room row + Chats tab dot (extends existing).
  • Contacts list: relay pin on the contact when any of their rooms has unread relay.
  • Contact detail: new Rooms section — one row per topic/room with its own pin.
  • Clear rule: open the room → pin clears. Exit → all pins clear.

What stays the same

  • Invite and register pins (accept invite, invite accepted) — unchanged.
  • Live L2 chat (accent bubbles) — no badges; only applies when you're in the room.
  • No phone/desktop push or app-icon badge in this change.

How it works

flowchart LR
  A[Wallet unlocked] --> B{Tab visible?}
  B -->|yes| C[Fast poll]
  B -->|no| D[Slow poll 30s]
  C --> E[sync + invites + relays]
  D --> E
  E --> F{New L1′ for room?}
  F -->|yes, not viewing room| G[Increment relay pin]
  F -->|viewing room| H[Suppress pin]
  G --> I[Chats + Contacts UI]

Out of scope

  • OS notifications / app-icon badge
  • L2 live message badges
  • Keeping Hyperswarm rooms connected in background
  • Remembering unread after wallet lock
